# Technology icon sources

The 22 technology tiles in `assets/stack/` embed artwork from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), pinned to revision [`c71c69e3a27e4436936b831c72643e6513b9f49b`](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/c71c69e3a27e4436936b831c72643e6513b9f49b). The Simple Icons project is distributed under CC0 1.0 Universal; its complete license is preserved in [simple-icons-CC0-1.0.txt](./simple-icons-CC0-1.0.txt).

Tile backgrounds, borders, type and layout are custom. Selected darker logo colors are lightened for contrast on the dark background; logo paths are unchanged. Brand names and marks identify the technologies used in the owner's projects and do not imply endorsement. Individual upstream icon metadata and any listed licenses are retained in `assets/stack/source-icons.json`.

Regenerate offline with `node scripts/build-stack.mjs`. The optional `--fetch` flag re-downloads the pinned upstream artwork and license; published SVGs make no external requests.
